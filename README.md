## 🧠 About Me
I am a doctoral researcher and research associate at **Helmut-Schmidt University, Hamburg**, working on advanced computational frameworks for fracture and damage in complex coupled systems.

My work focuses on bridging **theory, numerical methods, and large-scale simulations** to address engineering problems such as:
- stress corrosion cracking  
- concrete behavior under fire  
- hydrogen-related material degradation

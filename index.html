About

My research focuses on how damage and fracture evolve in materials under coupled physical processes. I develop computational frameworks that combine nonlinear finite element methods, partitioned solvers, and multiscale thinking for applications such as stress corrosion cracking and concrete structures under fire.

I also teach tutorial sessions in continuum mechanics (in english) and MATLAB programming (in german), and I enjoy building clean, reproducible simulation workflows that scale well from prototype studies to research-grade computations.

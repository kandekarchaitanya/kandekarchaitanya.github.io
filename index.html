About

My research explores how fractures initiate and evolve in complex materials and structures under coupled physical fields. I build partitioned and multiscale computational frameworks that connect nonlinear finite element methods with practical engineering applications such as stress corrosion cracking and concrete under fire.

Alongside research, I have delivered tutorial lectures in continuum mechanics and MATLAB programming, and I enjoy developing reproducible simulation pipelines that are both numerically robust and usable in larger research workflows.

Research profile

Computational mechanics for phase-field fracture and multiphysics coupling

I develop computational frameworks for damage, crack propagation, and coupled field problems, with applications spanning stress corrosion cracking, concrete under fire, and partitioned simulation workflows.

01

Current focus

Core themes that define my present research direction

Phase-field fracture in coupled environments

Electro-chemo-mechanical and thermo-hydro-mechanical settings for realistic crack evolution studies.

Partitioned simulation frameworks

Coupling specialized solvers to handle multiphysics and multiscale behaviour with flexibility.

Engineering-driven applications

Hydrogen systems, stress corrosion cracking, and concrete structures under fire.

01

Summary

Focused on computational fracture, multiphysics modelling, and reproducible scientific workflows.

My research investigates how damage and fracture evolve in materials under strongly coupled physical processes. I work on phase-field formulations, nonlinear finite element methods, and partitioned coupling strategies that make large, demanding simulations more reliable and easier to extend.

Alongside research, I teach tutorial sessions in continuum mechanics and MATLAB programming, and I enjoy building simulation pipelines that stay clean from prototype studies to research-grade computation.
